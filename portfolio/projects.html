Aria(1)
active
TYPE Locally Developed Transformer LLM

Aria is a custom transformer, architected and trained from the ground up rather than fine-tuned on top of an existing model. It's built in PyTorch with Hugging Face's training tooling doing the heavy lifting — the point isn't reinventing the plumbing, it's designing and understanding the architecture itself: attention, feed-forward blocks, the training loop, all deliberate choices rather than defaults inherited from a pretrained checkpoint. There's a working model running end to end already, and the current phase is training it further — refining it rather than proving the design works.
